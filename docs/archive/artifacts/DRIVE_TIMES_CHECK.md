# Drive times to confirm

The planner only considers drives up to 2 hours, so only pairs that could matter are listed (78 of 153). Each is the app's **estimate**: straight-line miles × 1.3 for roads, at an average 45 mph. When two stores are on opposite banks of the Columbia River, the estimate routes through the nearest bridge instead. The 18 store coordinates were read against where each town actually is and all fall in the right place; they are town-centre points, not the store doors, so very short pairs can be off by a few minutes.

**These are estimates, not looked-up road times.** No map service was reachable from the build environment, so none of these has been checked against real driving directions. To confirm one: look it up in Maps, then enter it under Setup → Stores → Drive times. A time you enter always wins and shows without the "~".

## Pairs I would check first

From general knowledge of these roads, not from a lookup. Treat as prompts to check, not as corrections.

- **Cathlamet ↔ Clatskanie**: the river is between them. The estimate goes through Longview and the Lewis & Clark Bridge. A ferry exists at Westport but is not counted.
- **Scappoose ↔ Woodland, Clatskanie ↔ Woodland, Cathlamet ↔ Scappoose**: also cross the river.
- **Cave Junction ↔ Rogue River**: the road runs through Grants Pass; I expect more than the estimate.
- **Florence ↔ Waldport**: a straight run up Highway 101; I expect less than the estimate.
- **Independence ↔ Silverton, Sheridan, Waldport**: Coast Range and valley roads are slower than a flat 45 mph.
- **John Day (Len's)** is more than 4 hours from every other store, so it never appears in a plan.
