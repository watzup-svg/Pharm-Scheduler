## 2. Replacing or editing?

**Replacing** the schedule screen, navigation and dashboard. About 3,000 lines of grid code went; about 1,700 came in.

Why it is shorter for the jobs:
- **1 Ready?** Header says it on every page. The overview's first two rows are the next two problems; each is a tap to the cell.
- **2 Place + undo.** Tap the OPEN cell (or a free name in "Next open shift") → done → toast Undo. One tap from the dashboard, two from the calendar.
- **3 Jump.** Today button, store chips with problem counts, Person picker (highlights their days, "Next Jane · 22").
- **4/5** Six-tab bar (phone) or top row (laptop). Coming back lands on the same store, cell and scroll.
- **6** Save is in the header. Open is the first item under File next to it.

Kept, on purpose: `placeName` is the only write gate, and every problem, tag and colour comes from `evaluate()`.

## Addendum: licences are a hard rule

Answers received: one person schedules and distributes each month (no shared editing needed); a pharmacist can only work in a state they are licensed in.

- A placement in a state the person is not licensed in is now handled like a name on a closed day: `placeName` refuses it (so the picker, paste, repeat, stamps and fills cannot put it there; stamps report it as skipped), and if one is already there (an older file, or a licence edited afterwards) it is a **hard problem** that blocks print. Clearing the name is the fix.
- The state comes from the store's address; a person with no licence recorded, or a store with no state, is not checked. The dashboard says how many pharmacists have no licence recorded when the stores span more than one state.
- Red dotted cells, a "licence" tag, a "N not licensed" flag on the store header, and a legend entry make it distinct from doubles (solid), holes (dashed) and closed days (hatched).
- In the day sheet and dashboard, people who cannot work at that store are listed last and cannot be picked ("Not licensed in WA").
- Weekday "usually can't work" stays a reminder only.
- Demo: Fenn Ritter (Oregon only) covering Wind River on the 13th is now a hard problem; the demo has 13 problems.

## Addendum: overrides, planned closures and more intelligence
Decisions from the district manager: accepting is one click (no reason needed); posters give the closure reason; licenses can never be accepted.
- **Accept instead of fix.** A hole, a person in two places, or a name on a closed day has an "Accept this" button in the day panel. Accepted problems stop blocking, count as "accepted" (not "to fix"), show as dashed gray "accepted" days, and are listed under "Accepted and planned" (District and overview) with a one-click Undo. An accept lasts only while the same problem is there: if the day is fixed and breaks again, it is a new problem. Accepts are saved in the file with the time.
- **Print is never stuck.** The Print page has "Accept N and keep going". The printed district page lists everything accepted or planned. Only license problems still block, and the page says why.
- **Close a store on purpose.** "Close <store> today…" (day panel, open-shift card, cover list, fill proposal) with a reason: Short-staffed, Weather or emergency, Other. Everyone scheduled there comes off the day (free to cover elsewhere). The poster says CLOSED with the reason; Holidays lists it as a "closure"; Reopen is one click.
- **First and last day** for each person (People form): days outside count as time off and are never suggested.
- **Fill the open shifts** (Tools, `/` search, overview): a proposal for every empty shift, best free person each, chosen in sequence so nobody is used twice in a day; untick any; nothing placed until you press the button; one undo.
- **Smarter hints:** fragile-week item when two or more days in a week have no spare pharmacist; hints before approving a request (who else is off, days it would leave with no spare); suggestions rank people who have already covered away from home a lot slightly lower; the cover list has "said no" so the next person comes up; posters changed since last printed say "Revised".
